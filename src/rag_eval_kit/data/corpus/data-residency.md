# Data Residency

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Regions

Larkspur stores workspace data in one of two regions: the United States (Oregon) or the European Union (Frankfurt). The EU region is available only on the Enterprise plan. All other workspaces are hosted in the US region.

## Choosing a region

The region is chosen when the workspace is created and cannot be changed later. To move to another region, create a new workspace in that region and import a full workspace export.
