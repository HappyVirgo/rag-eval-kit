# Invoices

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## When invoices are issued

Larkspur generates invoices on the first day of each billing period. Monthly subscribers receive an invoice on the 1st of every month. Every invoice is emailed to the billing contact and can be downloaded as a PDF from Settings > Billing > Invoice history.

## Currencies

Invoices can be issued in USD, EUR, or GBP. The currency is fixed when the first paid subscription starts and can only be changed by contacting support.

## Payment terms

Card payments are charged immediately when the invoice is issued. Enterprise customers who pay by bank transfer get net-30 payment terms, meaning the invoice is due 30 days after it is issued.
