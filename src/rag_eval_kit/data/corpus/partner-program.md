# Partner Program

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Who can join

Consultancies and agencies that set up Larkspur for their clients can apply to the partner program. Approved partners get a partner dashboard that shows every client workspace they manage.

## Partner benefits

Partners receive a 20% reseller discount on the subscriptions they resell and a free internal Business workspace with up to 10 seats. Partners also get early access to beta features and a private partner channel for support questions.

## Billing for partner-managed workspaces

A partner can pay for a client workspace directly, in which case invoices go to the partner, or let the client pay, in which case the client is the billing contact.
