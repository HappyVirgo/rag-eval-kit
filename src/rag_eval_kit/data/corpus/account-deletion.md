# Deleting a Workspace or Account

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Deleting a workspace

Only the workspace owner can delete a workspace, from Settings > General > Danger zone. Deletion has a 72-hour grace period during which the owner can cancel it. After the grace period the workspace is removed and cannot be recovered.

## Deleting a personal account

Users can delete their own account from Profile > Account. If the user is the owner of a workspace, they must transfer ownership or delete the workspace first. Tasks created by a deleted user remain in the workspace and are attributed to "Deleted user".
