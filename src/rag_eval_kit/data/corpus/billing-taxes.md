# Taxes and VAT

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Sales tax and VAT

Larkspur collects VAT from customers located in the European Union and the United Kingdom. Sales tax is collected in US states where Larkspur is required to do so. Tax is shown as a separate line on every invoice.

## Tax IDs and reverse charge

Business customers can add a VAT number or other tax ID under Settings > Billing > Tax information. When a valid EU VAT number is provided, invoices use the reverse-charge mechanism and no VAT is charged by Larkspur. Tax IDs added after an invoice is issued are not applied retroactively.
