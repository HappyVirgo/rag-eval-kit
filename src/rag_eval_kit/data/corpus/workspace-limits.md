# Workspace Limits

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Projects and tasks

The Free plan allows 2 active projects. The Team plan allows up to 500 active projects, and Business and Enterprise have no project limit. Any single project can hold up to 20,000 tasks; archived tasks do not count toward this limit.

## Storage

Storage is pooled across the workspace. Free workspaces get 1 GB in total. Paid plans include 10 GB of storage per seat. Additional storage can be bought in 100 GB blocks for $10 per month.
