# Refunds and Credits

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Refund policy

Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full if you cancel within 14 days of the purchase date. Monthly subscriptions are not refundable, but you keep access until the end of the current month after cancelling.

## Downgrades and credits

When you downgrade from a higher plan to a lower plan in the middle of a billing period, the unused portion is converted into an account credit. Credits are applied automatically to future invoices and never expire. Credits cannot be withdrawn as cash.
