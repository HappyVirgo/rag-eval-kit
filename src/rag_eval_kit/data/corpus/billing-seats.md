# Seats and Guests

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Adding and removing seats

Adding a seat in the middle of a billing period is prorated: you pay only for the remaining days in the period. Removing a seat takes effect at the start of the next billing period, so you are not refunded for the current one.

## Guest users

Guests are external collaborators who can only see the projects they are invited to. Each paid seat includes up to 5 free guests. Guests beyond that allowance are billed as regular seats. Guests cannot create projects or invite other people.
