# Support and SLA

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Support channels

Free workspaces get help through the community forum. Team workspaces get email support with a first response within 48 hours. Business workspaces get email and chat support with a first response within 24 hours. Enterprise workspaces get a dedicated support manager and a 4-hour first response for priority 1 incidents.

## Uptime SLA

Enterprise contracts include a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. If uptime falls below the SLA, the customer receives service credits of 10% of the monthly fee for each 0.1% below the target, up to 50%. Other plans do not include an SLA.
