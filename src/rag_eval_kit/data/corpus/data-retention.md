# Data Retention

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Trash

Deleted tasks and projects stay in the trash for 30 days and can be restored by any admin. After 30 days they are permanently deleted.

## After cancellation

When a workspace cancels its subscription, its data is kept for 90 days in case the customer returns. After 90 days all workspace data, including backups, is permanently deleted.

## Backups

Larkspur takes encrypted backups every 6 hours and keeps them for 35 days. Backups are used for disaster recovery only and cannot be used to restore individual tasks on request.
