Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

Larkspur publishes live system status at status.larkspur.example. The status page shows the health of the web app, the API, webhooks, and integrations separately.

You can subscribe to incident updates by email, RSS, or a Slack channel. Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 72 hours in advance and is performed on Sundays between 02:00 and 04:00 UTC.

Post-incident reports are published within 5 business days for any incident that affected more than 5% of API requests.
