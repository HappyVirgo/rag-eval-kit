# Plans and Pricing

> Synthetic documentation for Larkspur, a fictional project-management SaaS. Not a real product.

## Available plans

Larkspur offers four plans: Free, Team, Business, and Enterprise. The Free plan supports up to 3 members and 2 active projects. The Team plan costs $12 per user per month. The Business plan costs $24 per user per month. Enterprise pricing is negotiated with the sales team and requires a minimum of 50 seats.

## Annual billing

Every paid plan can be billed monthly or annually. Annual billing gives you two months free, which works out to roughly a 16.7% discount compared with paying monthly. You can switch from monthly to annual billing at any time from Settings > Billing.

## Trials

New workspaces can start a 14-day trial of the Business plan without entering a credit card. When the trial ends, the workspace drops to the Free plan unless a payment method has been added.
